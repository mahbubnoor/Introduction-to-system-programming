Lab 8.1 · Processes

Present lecture 8.1 Slides (PDF)
  1. TODO 1 split the line into argv with strtok; end it with NULL
  2. TODO 2 fork; in the child execvp, or print the error and _exit(127)
  3. TODO 3 waitpid in the parent and report how the child ended
  4. TODO 4 cd as a built-in with chdir
  5. TODO 5 a trailing &: do not wait; collect later with WNOHANG

the finished shell (real session)

mysh> echo hello from a child
hello from a child
mysh> cd /tmp
mysh> pwd
/tmp
mysh> nosuchprog
mysh: nosuchprog: No such file or directory
[369 exited with status 127]
mysh> ./segv
[370 killed by signal 11]
mysh> sleep 1 &
[371 running in the background]
mysh> echo waiting
waiting
[371 done]

Think: why can cd not be a program like ls? (Hint: whose current folder would change?) Answer in your report.

Lab 8.2 · execve and Scheduling

Present lecture 8.2 Slides (PDF)
