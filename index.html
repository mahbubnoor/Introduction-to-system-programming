$ gcc hello.c && ./a.out → assembler · linker · loader · ELF · kernel · system calls · memory · files · interrupts · drivers

How to use

Slides: →/Space next step, ← back, F full screen, a number and Enter to jump, S slide list. The bar on top switches lectures ([ ]) and opens the lab (L). Press ? for all keys.

Labs: every lab page has the tasks, all the files and tools that run in the browser. Its bar goes back to the slides, or to the previous or next lab. Then do the lab on a Linux PC.

Offline: everything is in this folder: fonts, videos, slides and labs. Copy it to a lab PC and open index.html in any browser, no internet needed.
