CSE 4105 · University of Barishal

System Programming

Lecture slides, explainer videos and lab files for the course taught by Md Mahbub E Noor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering. Fifteen lectures, from a SIC instruction to a USB keyboard driver.

$ gcc hello.c && ./a.out  →  assembler · linker · loader · ELF · kernel · system calls · memory · files · interrupts · drivers

How to use this page

  • Present plays the slides in your browser with every animation step: click, tap or use the arrow keys. The PDF shows each slide in its final state.
  • Watch the short video for each lecture; there is no sound, the captions explain each step.
  • Before each lab, open its interactive lab page: the tasks, the files, and tools to try in the browser. Then do the lab on Linux.

What you need for the labs

A Linux PC (Ubuntu 24.04 in our lab) with gcc, make, gdb, strace, nasm; from Lecture 7 on also qemu-system-x86, and for Lectures 14–15 the kernel headers. See the README.

Homework

Each lecture ends with a homework slide. Submit your answers to the course teacher; solutions are discussed in class and are not published here.
